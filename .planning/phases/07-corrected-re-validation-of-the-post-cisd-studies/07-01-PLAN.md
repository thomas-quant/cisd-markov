<objective>
Add an explicit reversal-barrier measurement to `post_cisd_context`'s `failed_gap_against` bucket (RES-04). A new sibling function to `barrier_hit_forward` returns a 3-way outcome — continuation / reversal / neither — over the identical `range(2, LOOKAHEAD + 2)` window (D-05, D-06). `compute_post_cisd_context` gains two new tags, `failed_gap_against_reversal` and `failed_gap_against_neither`, scoped strictly to the `failed_gap_against` bucket (D-07) and left as flat dict keys so the downstream manifest machinery needs zero changes.

Purpose: Today `barrier_hit_forward` collapses "stop hit first" and "window exhausted" into the same `False`, so a real reversal (candle[0]'s opposite extreme hit first) is indistinguishable from "went nowhere." This plan measures the reversal rate distinctly, which is the RES-04 deliverable and one of the diagnostic signals Phase 8's modeling decision depends on.

Output: `barrier_outcome_forward` in `cisd_barriers.py`; two new outcome tags on `compute_post_cisd_context`; an extended `_TAGS` list in `chart_post_cisd_context`; fast synthetic unit tests.
</objective>
