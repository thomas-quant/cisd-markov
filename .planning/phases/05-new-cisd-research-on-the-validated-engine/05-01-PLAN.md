<objective>
Ship RES-01: a `candle[1]` follow-through barrier study on the refactored engine, reporting through the validation harness (n + Wilson CI, IS/OOS) from the start.

The study answers: does the bar after a CISD (`candle[1]`) predict continuation? It buckets every CISD event by how `candle[1]` closes — the exact forward-analog of `compute_wick` (which compares `candle[0]`'s close to `candle[-1]`'s wick), moved one bar forward (`candle[1]`'s close vs `candle[0]`'s wick). For each bucket it measures the existing `candle[0]` target-before-stop barrier outcome in TWO windows side by side:
- **in-window** — `barrier_hit` unchanged (lookahead over `candle[1]`+`candle[2]`), directly comparable to the README but partly tautological (the conditioning bar sits inside the measured window),
- **re-anchored forward** — same `candle[0]` target/stop levels but the lookahead clock starts at `candle[2]` (`idx+2`), leakage-free and the honest "predict continuation" read.

Reporting both makes the tautology gap visible.

Purpose: Begin the new-research phase with an honest, harness-native study so no in-sample-only finding is ever produced. RES-01 hit-rates stay loosely comparable to published README numbers because the R-unit (`candle[0]` high/low) is unchanged (D-01).
Output: New precomputed feature columns, a `compute_candle1_followthrough` + `chart_candle1_followthrough` pair, registry entries, a re-export, harness wiring, and locking unit tests — all additive.

Candle indexing convention: `candle[-1]` = bar before the CISD (`prev_*`), `candle[0]` = the CISD bar (where `cisd_type` fires), `candle[1]` = next bar, `candle[2]` = two bars after.
</objective>
