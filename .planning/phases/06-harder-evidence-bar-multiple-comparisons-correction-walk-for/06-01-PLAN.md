<objective>
Introduce per-bucket significance testing and a Benjamini-Hochberg (FDR) multiple-comparisons correction into the validation harness, satisfying MHT-01. No hypothesis test or p-value exists anywhere in the current harness today — `determine_verdict()` only compares point estimates to 0.5 — so this plan introduces significance testing for the first time (D-01) and then layers one global BH correction over the full bucket grid (D-02) at the discovery-manifest stage only (D-03).

Purpose: A bucket's "confirmed" status must reflect how many buckets were tested across the whole engine, not just its own single-bucket Wilson CI. This is the "harder evidence bar" for the multiple-comparisons half of Phase 6.
Output: Additive `p_value`, `bh_rank`, `bh_q_value`, `bh_significant`, and `corrected_pass` columns on `validation_manifest_discovery.csv`, plus pure-Python significance/correction helpers and their characterization tests. Existing manifest columns are untouched.
</objective>
