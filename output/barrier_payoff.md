# Barrier-trade payoff of the frozen geo-significant buckets

Same trade as the rate test: entry close[t+k], stop = CISD opposite extreme (-1R), target = CISD near extreme, LOOKAHEAD bars, else marked at close. Net = gross minus an assumed round-trip cost of 0.70 pt (NQ) / 0.58 pt (ES): 1 tick slippage per side + ~$4 commission. `lift` = mean R minus the corridor-bin x ATR-regime baseline mean R. CIs are 95%, cluster-robust (session day / week, NQ+ES pooled). The buckets nest and overlap; rows are not independent.
