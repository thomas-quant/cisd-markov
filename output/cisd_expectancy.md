# CISD Single-Barrier Expectancy (R-multiples)

Each CISD is treated as a one-barrier trade: entry at the CISD close, the **only** barrier is the CISD invalidation (low for bullish, high for bearish), and `1R = |entry - invalidation|`. A stopped trade locks at `-1R`; otherwise the position is marked-to-close in R units. Upside is left open. `mean_r` is the per-trade expectancy.

- Forward horizon: 1..7 bars (headline tables show the terminal `h=7` bar).
- `stop_rate` = % of trades whose invalidation was breached within the window.
- `win_rate` = % of trades with R > 0 at the horizon.
- Events without a full forward window (data end) or with zero risk are excluded.
- SMT cases: included.
